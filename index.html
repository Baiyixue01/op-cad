Op-CAD: Benchmarking and Investigating Operation-oriented CAD Generation

Op-CAD is a research project on operation-oriented CAD generation, designed to support benchmarking, analysis, and reproducible evaluation for CAD modeling workflows.

Overview

About the project

This website serves as the public landing page for the paper Op-CAD: Benchmarking and Investigating Operation-oriented CAD Generation.

The goal is to provide a stable project home for future releases of processing pipelines, dataset documentation, models, training manifests, and benchmarking code.
